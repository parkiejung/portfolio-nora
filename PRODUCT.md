# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Hiring managers and recruiters evaluating Nora (Nga) for product design roles, plus potential freelance/contract clients. They're skimming quickly against a shortlist — they need to size up credibility and depth of thinking fast, then have a clear path to reach out or book a call.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio site showcasing Nora's product design case studies (Shopify apps, Circle K loyalty app redesign, a recycling-engagement app) so a hiring manager or client can judge her design thinking and reach out. Success = a visitor reads at least one case study and contacts her.

## Brand Personality
Warm, personal, craft-driven. The signature font, polaroid-style portrait, and hand-drawn card-stack hero already signal a maker who brings personal warmth into rigorous product work — not a cold agency deck.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS-template portfolio aesthetics: hero-metric blocks, gradient text, identical icon+heading+text card grids, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section.

## Design Principles
- Warmth without sacrificing credibility — personal touches (handwriting, photo, playful hero) coexist with clear evidence of rigor (case study structure, methods, findings).
- Show, don't just tell — case studies carry the proof; the homepage is a fast, honest preview, not a sales pitch.
- One clear next action per screen — reaching out should never require hunting.
- Respect the reader's time — scannable structure over dense paragraphs, since recruiters skim.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA baseline: sufficient contrast, visible focus states, keyboard operability, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation.
